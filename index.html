Following your computer’s local clock

Make yourself at home.

Walk
WASD or arrow keys. Hold Shift to move faster. On a touch screen, use the left joystick.
Look around
Drag the scene with your mouse or finger. The right touch joystick turns your view horizontally and vertically. Q and E turn with the keyboard; R and F look up and down. Mouse look captures the pointer; Escape releases it.
Go somewhere
Click a clear spot on the floor to walk there. The floor plan also takes you directly to a gallery.
Look closer
Click a artwork, or face it and press Enter. Use the Collection for a keyboard-accessible list. Zoom in to inspect details; Escape closes the viewer.
Stairs & floors
Walk up the right-hand switchback stairs to floors two and three. The floor plan provides direct room shortcuts.
Automatic tour
Start tour visits all 30 rooms, climbs both staircases, and returns to floor one. Pause and Resume keep your place. Walking or opening a photo or book pauses the tour. Choose 0.5×, 1×, 2×, or 4× speed.
Art albums
Click a book spine behind the favorites wall, or choose Albums. Click the left or right page to turn, or use the buttons or left/right arrows. Finish the last spread to return the book; Escape closes it early.
A new visit
Artworks stay in place while you explore, including after a refresh. New visit reshuffles them within each theme.

Artworks are linked from the owner’s Cloudinary folders.
